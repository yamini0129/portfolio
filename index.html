CI/CD and build modernisation

          
HarnessGradleJenkinsKubernetesJDK 17

          

            
The challenge
Older deployment and build tooling slowed releases and made them harder to trust.

            
What I did
Moved deployments from UCD to Harness, builds from Maven to Gradle and pipelines from Jenkins to EPLX. Upgraded Spring and JDK 17, and supported the PCF to OCP (Kubernetes) move with deployment orchestration and health checks.

            
The outcome
More reliable releases and faster developer feedback, recognised by clients.
